                  Appearance
                  yours alone — saved on this device, not shared with the household

                  Everyone in the house can pick their own look and their own celebration — these two don't follow the board, they stay on whichever phone or computer you set them on. If your device is set to reduce motion, the animation is skipped automatically.

                  Sections
                  switch off anything your family doesn't use

                  Unticking something only hides it — nothing is deleted, and switching it back on brings everything with it. These choices are shared by the whole household, so everyone sees the same board.

                  Currency
                  used for grocery prices and anything else with a cost

                  Changing this sets the currency for new entries. Prices already saved keep the currency they were entered in, so nothing gets silently relabelled — anything in a different currency is shown converted alongside the original.

                  Import a recipe

                  Three ways in — pick whichever suits where the recipe is. Nothing saves until you've looked it over.
                  From a recipe link
                  Best for normal recipe sites — most publish their recipe in a form the app reads directly, so it's exact and costs nothing. Instagram and TikTok links can't be read by any app; use one of the options below for those.
                  From pasted text
                  Copy the caption or the recipe text and paste it here. This is the best route for Instagram and TikTok — cheaper than a screenshot and more accurate too.
                  From screenshots
                  For when the text can't be selected or copied. Up to 3 screenshots of one recipe — pick them in order if it runs over more than one.
